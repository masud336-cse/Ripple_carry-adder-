Effect of Carry Propagation on N-Bit Adder Delay

Why Ripple Carry Adders become a bottleneck for 16-bit, 32-bit, and 64-bit ALUs (T_RCA = 2n·τg vs. Carry Lookahead O(log n)).

Mathematical Delay Breakdown (Current τg = 5 ns):
  • • Pᵢ = Aᵢ ⊕ Bᵢ and Gᵢ = Aᵢ · Bᵢ: Ready at 1 τg (all stages in parallel)
  • • Carry Cᵢ₊₁ = Gᵢ + (Pᵢ · Cᵢ): Adds +2 τg per stage (AND + OR gate)
  • • 4-Bit Worst-Case Carry (C₄): 2 × 4 × 5 ns = 40 ns (8 τg)
  • • 32-Bit RCA Worst-Case Delay: 2 × 32 × 5 ns = 320 ns (64 τg)
